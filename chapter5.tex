\section{Conclusion}
This study successfully designed, developed, and evaluated a solar-powered rental station with detachable power sources as an alternative energy solution for communities experiencing frequent power interruptions. The system effectively integrates solar energy harvesting, battery storage, and DC-to-AC conversion to deliver reliable electrical power for essential household appliances.

Based on the results, the developed prototype demonstrated stable performance in terms of power generation, system responsiveness, and communication efficiency. The integration of the ESP32 microcontroller, GPS tracking, and SMS notifier enabled accurate real-time monitoring, secure operation, and reliable transmission of system data. Furthermore, the implementation of a coin-operated and mobile application-based rental mechanism proved to be effective in managing transactions and improving accessibility for users.

The system was also found to be user-friendly and suitable for community use, as evidenced by usability testing results. Additionally, load analysis confirmed that the system can support small-to-medium electrical loads, making it practical for essential household applications during power interruptions.

Moreover, the study highlights the economic feasibility of the system, with a relatively short return on investment period, making it a viable solution for long-term implementation. The integration of renewable energy further contributes to environmental sustainability by reducing dependence on fossil-fuel-based generators.

However, certain limitations were identified, including dependency on solar availability, limited capacity for high-demand appliances, and reliance on internet connectivity for system operation. Despite these constraints, the study concludes that the proposed system is an effective, reliable, and sustainable solution that addresses the need for accessible and affordable backup power in underserved communities.

\section{Recommendations}

Based on the findings and limitations of the study, the following recommendations are proposed to further enhance the system:

The system may be improved by increasing battery capacity and optimizing solar panel efficiency to support higher loads and longer usage time.
Additional payment options, such as digital wallets like GCash, may be integrated to provide more convenient and flexible transactions.
Future designs may include offline functionality or backup communication methods to ensure continuous operation during internet interruptions.
Security features such as GPS tracking, alarms, or user authentication may be enhanced to prevent theft and unauthorized use.
Long-term field testing is recommended to evaluate system performance, durability, and user acceptance in real-world conditions.

Overall, these recommendations aim to improve the system’s performance, usability, and sustainability.
