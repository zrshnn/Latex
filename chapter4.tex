\chapter{RESULTS AND DISCUSSION}

{\baselineskip=1.2\baselineskip

This chapter presents the results and discussion of the testing and evaluation conducted on the developed Solar-Powered Rental Station with Detachable Power Sources. The findings are organized into three major components: system testing, mobile application testing, and hardware testing. System testing focuses on the overall functionality and integration of the system, including rental transactions, data processing, and communication between components. Mobile application testing evaluates the performance, usability, and responsiveness of the user interface, including account management, rental requests, and real-time monitoring features. Hardware testing examines the operation of key physical components.

The results obtained from these tests are analyzed in relation to the objectives of the study to determine the system’s effectiveness, reliability, and efficiency in providing a sustainable and accessible power solution. Furthermore, this chapter discusses the system’s strengths, identifies encountered issues, and highlights areas for improvement based on the observed performance during testing.
